### Account registration and password issues

- After registering, check your email promptly; a verification email will arrive. If it does not, check your spam folder or refresh.
- If an invitation link shows "Invalid activation code" within 10 minutes of receiving it, it may be a system bug. In that case, try logging in to Phira with the account and password you registered.
- To change your password, go to [Reset password](https://phira.5wyxi.com/reset-password). After clicking the link and entering your email, a password-reset link will be sent to your inbox; open it to change the password. A blank screen after entering your email is a known bug. If you do not receive the reset link within 5 minutes, try submitting the reset request again.

### Online service issues

- If chart loading or downloading fails, check whether the app is the latest version[+version-check]. If it is already the latest version, then it is a server issue, or your access is being restricted due to frequent requests; it will recover after a while.
- If chart scores cannot be uploaded and you see: `! Still uploading score`, it means the server's network condition is poor at the moment. If you do not care about the score, we suggest playing in offline mode.
- If you see: `This play does not count`, it means the score was judged invalid by the system due to local settings or actions and will not be counted or uploaded. Check the following items one by one; in most cases you can locate the cause:
  1. **MOD enabled[+mod]**: including but not limited to modified judgment, auto-play, invincibility, and other cheating features; the system will directly disqualify the score.
  2. **Keyboard mode enabled[+non-touch]**: when playing with a keyboard or other non-touch input devices, the score will not be counted.
  3. **Offline mode enabled**: in offline mode, scores cannot be uploaded to the server, so they are not counted in the rankings.
  4. **Speed-change enabled**: after enabling speed change in Settings, the score of that play will not be counted.

### About RKS[+rks]

- Phira's RKS mechanism is similar to the ptt mechanism in early Arcaea; RKS can go up or down.
- Currently only **some listed charts** can increase RKS.
- A chart must first pass normal review, then the uploader applies for listing; the listing review is voted on by reviewers, and only after 4 votes pass is the chart marked as stable and ranked. Only from this step do scores count toward RKS.

### Touch drop issues on devices

- iOS: Enable Guided Access.
- Android: Put the app into the system's built-in game space, or turn off all multi-finger gestures and other assistive features.
- General: Turn off aggressive optimization and enable low graphics quality; an overheated device causes CPU throttling and hurts performance; a dirty screen also affects touch. If touch drops persist[+touch-drop], check whether the screen supports multi-touch.

### Custom chart issues

- If you want to find custom charts[+custom-chart] for a specific song, the fastest way is to search on Bilibili and download them under the chart author's guidance;
- Uploading custom charts: before uploading a chart, please make sure you have carefully and fully read, understood, and agreed to all terms and contents of the [Phira Chart Upload Guidelines](https://docs.qq.com/doc/DU2dUaEt5WnFJV2Zh) and the [Phira Chart Content Policy (for charters)](https://docs.qq.com/doc/DU1dISHdEb0NuYVpB)[+charter]! In addition, **all of Phigros's exclusive songs, original artwork, charts, and enhanced charts based on official charts[+copyright] are prohibited from being uploaded; violators may have their accounts banned.**
- For non-exclusive songs, do not use original artwork when uploading; just make sure your chart design differs from the official chart and it may be uploaded. For other questions, the chart upload guidelines have detailed descriptions.

### How to delete your account

- You can currently submit an account deletion request in `In-game -> top-right avatar -> Delete account`; after you submit the request, the dev team will manually delete the account data.
- To cancel a deletion request, log in on the [official website](https://phira.moe) and you can cancel the request.

::::: danger Note
Because the official website currently does not support 好游快爆 one-click login, if you tap the delete account button by mistake, please send an email to `contact@phira.cn` with your username and user UID to submit a cancellation request!

[+phigros]: A falling-note rhythm game of the same type as Phira, developed by Pigeon Games. Phira's gameplay, chart system, and judgment rules all come from it.
[+rhythm-game]: Short for rhythm game. Phira is a "falling-note" game, meaning notes fall from above toward the judgment line and are hit when they reach it.
[+rks]: RKS: Phira's overall player rating, which quantifies your performance on listed charts into a single number.
[+rks]: Arcaea and ptt: another falling-note rhythm game and its overall rating (Potential); the RKS mechanism is modeled after it.
[+rks]: Listing and rankings: after a chart passes review it officially enters the online chart library; only scores from listed charts that count toward RKS enter the rankings.
[+rks]: Normal review: the first review a chart goes through after being uploaded to the online library.
[+rks]: Reviewer: community reviewers who vote on chart listings; only after 4 votes pass is the chart marked as listed.
[+mod]: MOD (modification): a third-party module that changes game behavior. Using any MOD in Phira disqualifies the current play's score.
[+mod]: Judgment: the system's ruling on whether each note was hit. "Modified judgment" means tampering with this ruling.
[+mp-server]: Private server: a multiplayer server set up by players themselves, not relying on the official server.
[+mp-server]: Server: the program that creates rooms and syncs scores during multiplayer; here it refers to community-compiled versions.
[+touch-drop]: Touch drop: when a finger is pressed on the screen but the device does not register the touch, causing missed notes or broken hold notes.
[+touch-drop]: Common causes: the in-game "aggressive optimization" and low graphics settings; device overheating causing CPU throttling; a dirty screen or a screen that does not support multi-touch.
[+resource-pack]: Resource pack: divided into charts and skins; a set of resources imported as zip/pez, no need to unzip.
[+resource-pack]: Skin pack: a resource pack that only changes the game interface's appearance.
[+custom-chart]: Custom chart: a chart made by a player and not officially published.
[+charter]: Charter: a player who makes charts, that is, the "chart author".
[+copyright]: Exclusive song: a song whose copyright is exclusively held by Phigros (or its rights holder) and which other works are not allowed to use.
[+copyright]: Artwork: the cover illustration of a song, usually with independent copyright.
[+copyright]: Official chart: a chart made by the official side (Phigros), as opposed to player-made custom charts. "Enhanced official charts" refer to versions rewritten on top of them, which are likewise prohibited from being uploaded.
[+device-req]: For Android, at least Android 7 is recommended for using online services; for Apple devices, at least iOS 12.
[+official-authorized]: 好游快爆 is an officially authorized download channel for Phira.
[+third-party]: A third-party download site, not an official channel.
[+recommended]: Apple accounts outside mainland China can download directly; recommended as the first choice.
[+overseas-source]: This download source is located overseas; access from mainland China may be slow or require a special network environment.
[+third-party-cn]: A third-party download site located in mainland China.
[+qq-email]: If you have a QQ account, you can directly use "QQnumber@qq.com" to register for Phira without registering another email.
[+version-check]: You can see the current version number in the top-left corner of the game's home screen.
[+non-touch]: Non-touch input, i.e. playing with a keyboard or other external devices.
[+special-network]: If it is inaccessible, a special network environment is required.
[+self-signed]: Self-signed, i.e. an IPA package you sign and install yourself.
[+import-fail]: For example: importing local charts / resource packs, etc. will all fail.
[+no-dup-group]: Please do not join the group repeatedly~
