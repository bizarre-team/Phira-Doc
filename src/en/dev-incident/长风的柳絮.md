# 长风的柳絮 (Willow Catkins in the Wind)

This incident took place at 18:53 on 2023-07-10, the time when sjfhsjfh sent the message "I'm here to take the blame" in the internal group chat.

## Incident Details

While manually operating the database with SQL statements to ban the user "长风的柳絮", sjfhsjfh accidentally sent an unfinished `UPDATE` statement without a `WHERE` clause and without properly setting `SET banned = TRUE`. As a result, every user's username was changed to "长风的柳絮".

> Recovering from logs took up Mivik's entire afternoon and evening study session, but fortunately it did not cause major data loss.

## Aftermath
